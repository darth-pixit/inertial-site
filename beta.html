Why not the store yet

The beta comes straight from us

Inertial is in closed beta. Our App Store and Google Play listings are in review, so for now the app reaches you directly.

On iPhone that happens through TestFlight, Apple’s own service for testing apps before launch. On Android it’s a file signed with our key, a standard way to test an app before it’s listed.

Android 7.0 or later

Install on Android

  1. Download. On your Android phone, tap Download for Android below. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open it. Tap the file when the download ends, or find it in Files under Downloads.
  3. Allow once. Android asks before a browser installs an app. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Install. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Sign in. Open Inertial and sign in. Allow location while you run, so your route records.
Download for Android

Play Protect warns because the app isn’t listed yet, not because of anything inside it. You can check the file is ours first.

If something gets in the way

“App not installed”
An older beta is usually still on the phone. Uninstall it and install again, and your runs come back when you sign in.
No switch to allow the install
On Samsung, open Settings, Apps, then Special access and Install unknown apps. On Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco, turn the switch on and wait out the short countdown.
Play Protect blocked it
Play Protect flags any app it hasn’t seen in Google Play yet. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
The download stopped
Try again on Wi-Fi. Nothing installs until the whole file arrives.

After it’s installed

You can turn Allow from this source off again. When a new build is ready, we email you a link, and installing it over the top keeps your runs.

When Inertial is listed, uninstall the beta first, then install from Google Play. Your runs sync to your account, so signing in brings them back.

We only link the file from inertial.run, and it downloads from our GitHub. If someone sends it to you anywhere else, don’t install it.

iPhone · iOS 15.1 or later

Install on iPhone

  1. Get TestFlight. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s free, and Apple makes it.
  2. Open your invite. On your iPhone, tap Join on iPhone below. TestFlight opens.
  3. Accept and install. Tap Accept, then Install. A small dot beside the name marks it as a beta.
  4. Sign in. Open Inertial and sign in. Allow location while you run, so your route records.
Join on iPhone

Each beta build runs for 90 days. New builds arrive in TestFlight on their own.

If something gets in the way

“This beta is full”
Testers join in batches. Reply to your invite email and we’ll add you to the next one.
“This beta isn’t accepting testers”
We’re between builds for a day or so. Try the link again tomorrow.
The link opens a web page
Install TestFlight first, then tap the link again on your iPhone.
The build expired
Open TestFlight and install the latest build. Your runs stay on your account.

Sending feedback

Take a screenshot in Inertial and TestFlight offers to send it to us with a note. You can also reply to your invite email.

When Inertial is listed, install it from the App Store. It replaces the beta, and your runs stay.

Is this safe

Yes, and here’s how to tell

TestFlight is Apple’s

Apple reviews a beta before its public link opens to testers.

The Android file is signed

It carries our key, and the fingerprints above let you check it.

Nothing to hand over

We never ask for a password or a payment to install.

Same privacy rules

The beta follows our privacy page. You can delete your account in the app.
