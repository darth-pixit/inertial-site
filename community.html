Community rules

Run kind. Post kind.

Signing in means you accept these rules and the terms of use. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people.

🚫

No abuse or hate. No harassment, threats, bullying or slurs about anyone’s race, religion, caste, gender, sexuality or body.

🔞

No sexual or violent content. No nudity, sexual material, gore or anything that encourages self-harm.

🏃

Real runs, real people. No fabricated runs, no pretending to be someone else, no spam or scams.

⋯

Report anything with ···. Every run, comment, story and profile has it. A report hides the content from you at once.

⏱

Reviewed within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove the accounts that post it.

🛑

Block works both ways. A blocked person can’t see you, and you won’t see them.

Obvious abuse is stopped before it posts. For anything else, write to safety@inertial.run.
