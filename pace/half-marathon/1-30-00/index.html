Racing smarter

What pace is a 1:30 half marathon?

1:30:00 for the half marathon is 4:16/km (6:52/mi), held the whole way. Halfway on even pace is 45:00. Most watches measure the course long, so the pace you actually need on the screen is nearer 4:12/km.

The pace in min/km and min/mi

1:30:00 over 21.0975 km (13.11 mi) is 4:16/km (6:52/mi). Run every kilometre 10 seconds slower than that and you finish 3:31 late, because 10 seconds times 21.0975 km is 3:31.

A 90-minute half is 4:16/km (6:52/mi) and it is the classic club-runner benchmark. It usually needs a 5K under 20 minutes and 50 to 70 km a week. The distinctive thing about this target is that the pace sits very close to threshold for most people attempting it, which means there is almost no margin for a bad first 5 km. Go out at 4:05/km and you will not be able to recover inside the distance the way you could in a marathon. The reliable execution is boring: first 5 km at 4:18, middle 10 km at 4:16, last 6 km on whatever is left. People who do that run 1:29 and people who chase it early run 1:33.

Halfway

Even pace puts you through halfway at 45:00. A 2% negative split is halfway at 45:54 and a second half of 44:06. More than 0:54 ahead of 45:00 is borrowed time, not a cushion. The idea is a negative split, planned even and only slightly negative if you run it at all.

The watch trace

On a half marathon, 1.5% of 1:30:00 is 1:21. The watch pace that still finishes in 1:30:00 is 4:12/km (6:46/mi). The longer explanation sits on the half marathon pace charts. Why the distance grows is GPS accuracy.

Questions runners actually ask

Is a 1:30 half marathon a good time?
The page answers the pace, 4:16/km (6:52/mi), not whether the time is yours. A recent half, or a 10K plus the volume behind it, is the evidence. The race predictor prints three models so you can see how far apart they sit.
Should I run even splits or negative splits?
Plan even at 4:16/km (6:52/mi). A 2% negative split on the half is a small gap at halfway, not a minute taken out of the first 5 km (3.11 mi).
