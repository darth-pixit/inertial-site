Racing smarter

What pace is a 2-hour half marathon?

2:00:00 for the half marathon is 5:41/km (9:09/mi), held the whole way. Halfway on even pace is 1:00:00. Most watches measure the course long, so the pace you actually need on the screen is nearer 5:36/km.

The pace in min/km and min/mi

2:00:00 over 21.0975 km (13.11 mi) is 5:41/km (9:09/mi). Run every kilometre 10 seconds slower than that and you finish 3:31 late, because 10 seconds times 21.0975 km is 3:31.

The two-hour half is the most popular target at the distance. It needs 5:41/km (9:09/mi), which is exactly the pace of a four-hour marathon and a useful thing to know: if this feels like a hard day, sub-four is not the next race. Most people who run it train three to four days a week at 30 to 40 km. The failure mode is the first two kilometres. Start-line adrenaline and a crowd moving at 5:20 pace pulls people through 2 km thirty seconds early, and at this fitness that debt is repaid with interest after 15 km. Let the first kilometre be slow. You will spend the rest of the race overtaking the people who did not.

Halfway

Even pace puts you through halfway at 1:00:00. A 2% negative split is halfway at 1:01:12 and a second half of 58:48. More than 1:12 ahead of 1:00:00 is borrowed time, not a cushion. The idea is a negative split, planned even and only slightly negative if you run it at all.

The watch trace

On a half marathon, 1.5% of 2:00:00 is 1:48. The watch pace that still finishes in 2:00:00 is 5:36/km (9:01/mi). The longer explanation sits on the half marathon pace charts. Why the distance grows is GPS accuracy.

Questions runners actually ask

Is a 2-hour half marathon a good time?
The page answers the pace, 5:41/km (9:09/mi), not whether the time is yours. A recent half, or a 10K plus the volume behind it, is the evidence. The race predictor prints three models so you can see how far apart they sit.
Should I run even splits or negative splits?
Plan even at 5:41/km (9:09/mi). A 2% negative split on the half is a small gap at halfway, not a minute taken out of the first 5 km (3.11 mi).
