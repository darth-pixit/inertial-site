Pace

Half marathon pace charts

The half marathon is 21.0975 km (13.11 mi). It is long enough that the first 10 km (6.2 mi) has to feel too easy. The people who fade after 16 km (10 mi) almost always ran the opening half at their 10K pace.

Each chart below is one finish time: the pace in min/km and min/mi, the full splits in both units, the halfway clock time on even pace, and the pace your watch has to show when its trace runs about 1.5% long. The times covered here are 1:30:00, 1:45:00, 2:00:00, 2:15:00, 2:30:00.

If your goal is not in that list, the pace calculator does any half marathon time with the same maths. The full set covers the other distances.
