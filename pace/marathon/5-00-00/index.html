Racing smarter

What pace is a 5-hour marathon?

5:00:00 for the marathon is 7:07/km (11:27/mi), held the whole way. Halfway on even pace is 2:30:00. Most watches measure the course long, so the pace you actually need on the screen is nearer 7:00/km.

What pace is a 5-hour marathon?

5:00:00 over 42.195 km (26.22 mi) is 7:07/km (11:27/mi), held from the first step to the last. There is no rounding slack in that number: run the first kilometre at 7:17/km and you have already spent ten seconds you will not get back cheaply.

A five-hour marathon is 7:07/km (11:27/mi), and a very large number of people finish within twenty minutes of it. Many of them run and walk deliberately, which is a strategy rather than a compromise: a planned four minutes running to one minute walking often produces a faster finish than continuous running for people at this pace, because it protects the last 10 km. The failure mode is heat and fuel, not speed. Five hours on course means you may start in the cool and finish in the sun, and it means five hours of eating. If you are training for this time, practise the exact walk breaks and the exact food. The pace itself you can already run.

Where should I be at halfway?

Even pace puts you through halfway at 2:30:00. Most people who finish this time went through slightly slower than that, not faster — a 2% negative split means halfway at 2:33:00 and a second half of 2:27:00.

The decision rule for race day: if you reach halfway more than 3:00 ahead of 2:30:00, you are not ahead of schedule, you are borrowing. Ease back to 7:07/km (11:27/mi) and hold it.

What if my watch measures the course long?

Almost every watch does. A marathon that reads 1.5% long on your wrist means you have to run 7:00/km by the watch to finish in 5:00:00 by the clock. That is the pace to put on the screen, and it is why pacing off a watch's average-pace field alone tends to leave people a minute or two short.

Questions runners actually ask

Is a 5-hour marathon a good time?
Good is a comparison, and it depends who with. Whether it is achievable for you is the answerable question: it comes down to a recent race, your weekly volume and your longest run. The race predictor puts numbers on all three.
Should I run even splits or negative splits?
Even, planned; slightly negative, executed. Going through halfway 1-2% slower than even pace costs almost nothing if you are strong at the end, and saves the race if you are not.
