Pace

10K pace charts

The 10K is 10 km (6.21 mi). It is where a pace that felt easy at 2 km stops feeling easy at 7 km. Most people who miss their time do it by running the first 3 km (1.9 mi) at 5K effort.

Each chart below is one finish time: the pace in min/km and min/mi, the full splits in both units, the halfway clock time on even pace, and the pace your watch has to show when its trace runs about 1.5% long. The times covered here are 40:00, 45:00, 50:00, 55:00, 1:00:00.

If your goal is not in that list, the pace calculator does any 10K time with the same maths. The full set covers the other distances.
