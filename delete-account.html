Delete your account

Leave whenever you like.

This page is for Inertial.run, the running app made by Parth Dixit. Delete your account in the app, or by email if you no longer have it.

📱

In the app. Settings, then Delete account. Everything is erased straight away.

✉️

Without the app. Email safety@inertial.run with the subject “Delete my account”. Send it from your account’s email, or include the phone number you sign in with.

🔒

We check it’s you. If we can’t match the request to your account, we reply and ask you to confirm. Nothing is deleted until then.

⏱

Done within 30 days. We delete the account by hand and email you when it’s gone.

What is deleted

Your profile and photo, runs and their photos, stories, comments, cheers, follows, challenges you joined, connected services and your sign-in. You can sign up again later, but nothing comes back.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Reports are kept only as long as needed to review them and act on them.
  • Anonymous usage events and crash reports were never linked to you.
Want a copy first? In the app, Settings, then Export everything.
