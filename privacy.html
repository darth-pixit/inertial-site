Privacy policy

How Inertial.run handles your data

Last updated 8 November 2026

Inertial.run is made by Parth Dixit, an independent developer in India ("we"). This policy covers the Inertial.run app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android and the services behind it. Questions, requests and complaints go to safety@inertial.run.

What we collect, and why

Data Why we use it
Account: your name, email address or phone number, from the way you sign in (Apple, Google or a phone code) To create your account and keep it secure
Waitlist: your email, a WhatsApp number if you add one, which link brought you, and whether you're bringing runs from another app To confirm you joined and tell you when your spot opens
Profile: handle, display name, profile photo, privacy settings, and confirmation that you are 18 or older (not your date of birth) To show your profile to the people you allow
Runs: GPS route, time, distance, pace, splits, elevation, titles and notes To record, save and show your runs
Heart rate (optional): read from Apple Watch through Apple Health, only if you allow it To show your heart rate during a run and save its average with the run
What you post: photos, stories, comments, cheers, follows, reports and blocks To share with the people you choose and keep the community safe
Imports and connections (optional): a data export you upload, or access to another running service you choose to connect To bring your past runs in. Disconnect any time
Anonymous usage events: for example "a run was saved", with a random device ID, run duration and distance. Never your route, location, name or account To learn which features work. Not linked to you
Crash reports: device model, system version and the error. Never coordinates, handles or sign-in tokens To fix crashes. Not linked to you

We do not collect your contacts, your advertising identifier, your browsing history or payment details. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Location

  • Location is used only while you record a run you started, including with the screen locked. Inertial.run never tracks you in the background otherwise.
  • Privacy zones hide the 200 m around places you choose, such as home, from every shared map. They are on by default.
  • Location data is removed from photos before they leave your phone.
  • Beacon, the live link, is off until you turn it on. While it is on, anyone with the link can see your position during that run. The link stops working an hour after you finish.

Health data

  • Heart rate is read through Apple Health (HealthKit) only with your permission. Runs recorded on Apple Watch are saved to the Health app as workouts.
  • Health data is used only to show and save your runs. It is never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, never sold, and never shared with anyone except the provider that stores your account.
  • Turn access off any time in iOS Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices.

Who can see what

  • You choose who sees each run: only you, your followers, or everyone. You can change it per run.
  • Stories disappear after 24 hours.
  • People you block cannot see your profile or your runs.

Service providers

These companies process data for us only to run their part of the service. Some are outside India, including in the United States.

  • Supabase: accounts, database and photo storage
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you use them
  • Twilio: phone sign-in codes
  • Resend: waitlist emails
  • Render: hosts Beacon live links
  • PostHog: anonymous usage events
  • Sentry: crash reports
  • OpenFreeMap: map images, which your phone loads directly
  • Strava: only if you connect your account to import your runs

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your data while you have an account.
  • Delete a run any time from the run's page.
  • Settings, then Delete account, permanently erases your profile, runs, photos, comments, cheers, follows and sign-in identity straight away. Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • No app? Email safety@inertial.run and we delete the account within 30 days. How to delete your account.
  • Waitlist signups are kept until your spot opens or you ask. Email safety@inertial.run to be removed.
  • Reports are kept only as long as needed to review them and act on them.
  • Anonymous usage events and crash reports are not linked to you.

Your rights

  • See and export: Settings, then Export everything.
  • Correct: edit your profile and runs in the app.
  • Erase: delete a run, or your whole account, with or without the app.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off location, motion, photos or Health access in your phone’s settings, or disconnect a connected service.
  • Complaints: write to safety@inertial.run. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

Inertial.run is for people aged 18 and over, and sign-up asks you to confirm your age. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn we have, we delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules let only the people you allow read your data, and photos load through short-lived links.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. We tell you in the app about any change that affects how your data is used.
