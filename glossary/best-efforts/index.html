Glossary

Best efforts

Your fastest recorded stretch over a standard distance, found anywhere inside a run. Inertial tracks 4 of them: 1 km, 5K, 10K and half marathon.

By Parth DixitPublished 1 September 2026

What it is

A rolling search through your run for the fastest continuous stretch of a standard distance. It does not need to start at the beginning.

If you ran 12 km (7.5 mi) and the quickest 5 km inside it took 24:10, that is your 5K best effort for the day.

DistanceWhat it usually reflects
1 kma hard interval or a finishing surge
5Ka parkrun, or a tempo block
10Ka race or a long tempo
half marathonalmost always an actual race

What it is not

It is not a race result. A best effort found mid-run started from a rolling start, on your route, measured by your watch. A race is a standing start on a certified course with an official clock.

It is also not comparable across watches, because the distance each one thinks you covered differs slightly.

What moves it

  • Racing. The obvious one.
  • Where you put the effort. A fast finish sets 1 km bests easily.
  • GPS quality. A noisy trace can flatter or spoil a best.
  • The route. Downhill stretches produce bests that do not transfer.

When to ignore it

Ignore a best effort set on a downhill or with a tailwind. It will not repeat.

Ignore small changes. A two-second improvement over 5 km is inside the measurement error of the watch.

Sources

  • Gilgen-Ammann R, Schweizer T, Wyss T. Accuracy of distance recordings in eight positioning-enabled sport watches. Sensors 20(19):5493, 2020 primary
  • World Athletics Competition Rules, Book C2.1 — record and course requirements reference
